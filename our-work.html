A local auto repair shop

Wil was hired full-time at a local auto repair shop for business development and marketing. Here's what he found and built there.

  • Vendor credits.

    Wil matched 11,139 purchase-order lines against billing and found vendor credits that weren't coming back. The leak is now stopped going forward. A person still checks the exceptions.

    $217Kstopped going forward

  • Parts pricing.

    Pricing each part instead of a blanket markup. The tool only flags. A person approves before anything changes.

    ~48%more profit on eligible parts

  • What was built.

    • One trusted record. Customers, cars, orders, calls, and history linked in one place, checked for freshness and tested. Every tool and AI answer works from it.
    • Caller screen. The front desk sees who's calling, their history, and why they're likely calling before picking up.
    • Morning shop brief. Every weekday: appointments, parts on hold, estimates waiting, and overdue declined work.
    • Customer follow-up. A list of customers who've gone quiet or are due for service, so nobody falls through.
    • Newsletter in the owner's voice. Tuned from his own edits.
    • Knowledge kept. Front-office chat sessions summarized into repeatable skills, and a technician research tool that returns step-by-step answers with sources. No outcome figures yet.

How it was built to be trusted

  • People approve.

    The parts pricing tool only flags. Nothing changes without a person reviewing it.

  • Every claim shows its source.

    AI customer summaries link each fact back to where it came from.

  • AI notes are labeled.

    Call notes are marked "AI generated" and can be deleted in one click.

  • Customer data stays separate.

    Checked across about 100,000 linked records: no customer's data showed up on another customer's record.

  • AI only where it fits.

    Some work moved from AI back to plain code where it had to be exact.

Sales pipeline

A pipeline that researches each prospect, drafts outreach in the salesperson's voice, tracks every lead's stage, and flags who's due for a follow-up each morning. A person reads and sends every message.

Research

A research helper that gathers the sources, pulls out the facts that matter, and writes a short brief where every claim links back to where it came from.

Marketing run by AI agents

Agents plan the calendar, draft posts and emails in the brand's voice, learn from the owner's edits, and report what performed. Nothing goes out without a person's OK.
