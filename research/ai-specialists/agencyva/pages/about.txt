ABOUT AGENCY VA
Built to help insurance agencies work better

Agency VA helps insurance agencies build and support modern teams through trained remote professionals, managed service teams, global employment infrastructure, and technology designed around insurance operations.

OUR STORY
Different teams use AVA for different parts of the operation

Agency VA began with Ben Barrientos and Wessly Anderson, two agency owners who understood the operational pressure behind running and growing an insurance business. What started in 2005 has since grown into a broader workforce and technology company built specifically around the needs of insurance agencies.

2005

The story begins

Ben Barrientos and Wessly Anderson start Agency VA, using their agency owner experience.

2012

Building a global workforce

Agency VA expands its model with trained remote professionals, supporting insurance agencies in client-facing and back-office work.

2018

AVA enters the tech

The company uses AVA technology for workforce management, tracking, and reporting.

Today

Workforce + technology

Agency VA combines virtual assistants, service teams, and AVA products to help insurance agencies hire and scale.

OUR MISSION & VALUES
Better support for agencies. Better opportunities for people.

Agency VA was built to solve real operational needs for insurance agencies while creating stable, well-supported careers for remote professionals. That balance continues to shape how we hire, support, and grow.

Our Values

We believe remote work should create real economic opportunity. That means treating people well, providing meaningful benefits, and helping our VAs build greater financial stability for themselves and their families.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower insurance agencies to cultivate robust teams while enhancing the well-being of the professionals who drive their success. We believe that growth should be mutually beneficial for all partners involved.
