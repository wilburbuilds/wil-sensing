BUILT-IN OVERSIGHT
BUILT-IN OVERSIGHT
AI assistance without giving up control of your core systems

AVA is designed around a clear division between what the software can surface or assist with and what still requires a person.

Your AMS remains the system of record

AVA works from a synchronized mirror of agency data. It does not overwrite the AMS directly.

Changes remain deliberate

If AVA identifies an issue such as a likely duplicate record, the merge is still completed by a person in the source system.

Live calls can be handed over

Licensed team members can take over supported live calls when the situation requires it.

Outbound calling is consent-aware

Campaigns account for consent, revocation, do-not-call status, pacing, and call history.

Recommendations include context

The product identifies issues like duplicates or gaps, providing reasoning behind results instead of unexplained suggestions.

Data freshness is visible

AVA identifies when synchronized information was last updated so users know the state of the data they are reviewing.
