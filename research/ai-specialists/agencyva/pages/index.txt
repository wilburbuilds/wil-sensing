About Wess
 

Wessly J. Anderson is the Co-Founder and President of Agency VA, where he's redefining how insurance agencies scale through elite Virtual Agents and AVA, his proprietary remote workforce platform.
 

As an entrepreneur and insurtech innovator, Wess partners with agencies nationwide to help them streamline operations, increase profitability, and embrace the future of work. A sought-after speaker and industry leader, he is committed to advancing insurance technology, fostering collaboration, and equipping agencies with the tools they need to grow in an increasingly digital world.

Beyond business, Wess is a devoted husband and proud father of four. He credits much of his entrepreneurial journey to his father, who introduced him to technology and worked alongside him for more than 25 years.
 

Guided by a passion for helping others succeed, Wess has built his career around creating opportunities and making a meaningful impact. When he's not leading Agency VA, you'll find him making memories with his family, snowboarding in the mountains of Utah or surfing the waves of Puerto Rico.
