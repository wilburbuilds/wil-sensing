AVA PRODUCT ECOSYSTEM
Three products.
One connected technology stack.
AVA Tracking

See how your remote team is working

AVA Tracking gives agencies visibility in time tracking, activity, screenshots, EOD reporting, and workforce analytics, helping managers understand how work is progressing without relying on constant check-ins.

ASK AVA

Give VAs faster access to the information they need.

ASK AVA helps virtual assistants find answers, reference internal information, and get guidance during day-to-day work, reducing the time spent searching through documents or waiting for another team member to respond.

AVA Agentic Ai

Bring agency data and AI-assisted workflows into one system.

AVA Agentic Ai connects clients, renewals, cross-sell, quotes, calls, campaigns, tasking, growth, and reporting in one environment, with specialized AVA roles supporting different parts of the operation.

AVA SECURITY FRAMEWORK
Security built into every layer of AVA

AVA’s security framework combines access controls, encryption, secure communication, and threat controls to protect agency data and personnel.

Security by Design. Compliance by Default.

Built into the foundation, not just added later on

Security and compliance controls are made part of the platform from the start, helping us maintain a consistent standard across our technology.

Secure Communication. Zero Phishing.

Reduce risk across all everyday communication channels

Secure communication controls are designed to help protect users from phishing attempts and other common message-based threats.

MFA & Conditional Access Enforced.

Make sure the right people get the right access

Multi-factor authentication and conditional access add additional checks before users can reach protected systems and information.

Compliant Endpoints, Always.

Always keep access tied to trusted devices for security

Endpoint controls help ensure that devices connecting to AVA meet the required security standards before accessing protected resources.

Intelligent Data Loss Prevention

Help keep sensitive information where it belongs

Data Loss Prevention controls help identify and reduce the risk of sensitive information being shared, moved, or exposed inappropriately.

Proactive & Robust Threat Reduction.

Address security risks before they become incidents

AVA’s security approach includes preventative controls designed to reduce exposure to common threats before they affect users or systems.

Mandatory Updates & Secure Browsing.

Keep the everyday browsing on your devices safer

Required software updates and secure browsing controls help reduce exposure to known vulnerabilities and unsafe web activity.

Data Encrypted. In Transit & At Rest.

Protect information while it moves and while it is stored

Encryption helps protect data both when it is being transmitted between systems and when it is stored within the platform.
