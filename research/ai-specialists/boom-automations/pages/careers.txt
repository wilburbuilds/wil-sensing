Careers

Build the systems that run real companies.

Boom is a small team deploying AI agent systems into mid-market operations: finance, revenue, and ops. Every engineer here ships into production, sits with the client, and owns the result.

Three open roles. All full-time.

How we work

Ship into production weekly

No sandboxes. Your work runs inside a client's real stack within the first month.

Sit with the operator

You'll be in the room with the CFO, the ops lead, the person whose week you're giving back.

Small team, large surface area

You'll touch discovery, architecture, build, deployment, and results reporting on every engagement.

Open roles

Forward Deployed Engineer
Full-time
Miami or remote (US)
2 open seats
What you'll do
Embed with our clients' teams. You'll work inside their operations, not from a distance, and own the deployment end to end: map the department, design the agent system, ship it into their stack.
Build with your preferred models and tools, whether that's Claude Code, Codex, or something else; integrate against ERPs, CRMs, and finance tools.
Run weekly working sessions with client operators and turn feedback into shipped changes within days.
Instrument what you ship and report the result back to the client: hours saved, process time cut.
What we look for
You've shipped production software that real users depend on.
You can code. TypeScript or Python, and you can read the rest.
You understand data: structures, databases, schemas, and how information moves between systems.
Hands-on with LLM APIs, agents, and tool calling.
Comfortable explaining a system to a COO in plain language.
Bias to ship: you'd rather deploy a v1 this week than a v3 next quarter.
Nice to have
Postgres or another relational database in production, plus APIs and webhooks.
You've worked directly with customers or operators before.
Lovable or a similar tool for shipping interfaces fast.

Build the simple automations that don't need an engineer.

You can read a workflow, an API doc, and a spreadsheet without help.
Organized to a fault. Nothing you own gets dropped.
Comfortable with Claude, Claude Code, and Lovable, or fast to pick them up.
You want to become an engineer here, and this is how you start.
