Ongoing Management & Enablement

A department doesn't stop changing when the project ends, so neither does the system. After launch we run it: we measure what the agents do, maintain the OS as your tools and rules change, and ship improvements on a cadence. Your team keeps getting our AI enablement training, updated every quarter, with access for life. Managed, not handed off.

Full observability on every agent, with evaluation loops that feed improvements back into the system
Agent behavior tuned as your operations and policies change
New workflows added to the same OS instead of new projects
Quarterly-updated enablement training that your team keeps for life
How it connects

This is how the OS compounds: every month it covers more of the department than it did the month before, and your team gets better at running it.
