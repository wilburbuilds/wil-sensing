OS Build-Out

The foundation of every Boom engagement. We design and deploy one AI-native operating system for the company, starting with a single department: a constant context layer that connects your data, your existing tools, and the agents that do the work. Each department we add runs on the same layer. Nothing depends on a person carrying information between apps.

One shared context across every workflow in the department
Integrates the tools worth keeping (ERP, CRM, finance, comms) and replaces the ones that aren't
Low-cost automations first, for the work that doesn't need AI and shouldn't pay for it
Permissions, logging, and audit trails from day one
How it connects

SaaS consolidation happens inside the OS, agents are deployed into it, and evaluation keeps it improving.
