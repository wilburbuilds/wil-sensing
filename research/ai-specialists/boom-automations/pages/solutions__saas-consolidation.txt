SaaS Consolidation

Most departments run on a dozen subscriptions that each do one thing and don't talk to each other. Once the OS exists, a lot of them stop being necessary. We audit the stack, fold what the system can do natively into it, and cut the rest.

Tool-by-tool audit of what the OS replaces, what stays, and why
Fewer seats, fewer integrations, fewer handoffs between apps
Data from retired tools migrated into the context layer, not lost
Savings tracked and reported alongside the hours saved
How it connects

Consolidation is a layer of the OS build-out, not a separate project. The agents we deploy run on the tools that remain.
