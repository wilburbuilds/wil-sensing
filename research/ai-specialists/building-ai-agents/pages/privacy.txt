PRIVACY NOTICE
How we handle your information.

Last updated July 22, 2026.

Information you submit

When you complete an application or contact us, we may receive your name, contact information, business details, and the information you choose to share about your operations.

How we use it

We use this information to evaluate whether there is a fit, respond to your inquiry, prepare for conversations, and improve how we serve prospective clients. We do not sell information submitted through the application.

Form provider and retention

The application may be delivered through Tally, which processes form responses on our behalf. We keep application information only as long as reasonably needed for evaluation, follow-up, business records, or legal obligations.

Sensitive information

Do not send passwords, private access keys, payment card information, protected health information, or other sensitive credentials through the application.

Questions or requests

To ask a privacy question or request an update or deletion, email us at hello@buildingaiagents.ai.
