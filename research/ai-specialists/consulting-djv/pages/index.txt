USUAL QUESTIONS
What is DJV Consulting?

DJV Consulting provides bilingual AI-financial services for LatAm and global SMBs. We blend senior management experience with AI and web technologies to help companies achieve sustainable financial growth, optimize CFO functions, and implement practical technology solutions.

What services do we offer?

Our main services are:  

Fractional CFO (monthly subscriptions with business diagnosis in 2–3 weeks)  

AI solutions for Finance & Supply Chain (ready-made or custom-built to maximize profitability and cash)

Strategy management, financial planning, and process optimization consulting

Who is DJV Consulting for?

We work with small and medium businesses (SMBs) and mid-market companies in LatAm and globally. Typical industries include automotive (autoparts & logistics), fintech, travel, supply chain (freights, warehouses, purchasing), software/services, and general SMBs seeking growth through better financial processes, or AI strategic implementations.

What is a Fractional CFO and how does it work?

Fractional CFO is a flexible monthly subscription model. We start with a business diagnosis (usually completed in 2–3 weeks), then provide ongoing strategic financial support, planning, and guidance so you can focus on running the business while we help optimize the rest.

What makes DJV Consulting different?

We combine 10+ years of senior management experience in global organizations with hands-on expertise as a tech entrepreneur specialized in AI and process optimization. The focus is short-term profitability and cash improvements through realistic, transparent implementations rather than theoretical advice.

Do you work in both English and Spanish?

Yes. We are fully bilingual and serve clients across LatAm and global markets in both languages.

How do you measure success / ROI?

We define clear KPIs and frameworks from the beginning. Success is measured by tangible improvements in profitability, cash flow, process efficiency, and strategic outcomes—not just activity metrics.

How can I get started?

Secure your free call through the form or CTA on consultingdjv.com. We will contact you to discuss your specific needs around finance, AI, websites, or strategy.
