SMB SUPPLY CHAIN · 2026 EDITION
METRIC 01 · THE PROBLEM
Why most SME AI projects burn cash.
 WRONG TOOL
Frontier LLMs used for tasks a $50/mo rules engine could solve.
WRONG ORDER
AI added before data and workflows are clean — confident wrong answers.
WRONG METRIC
Buying tech instead of buying outcomes. No baseline, no ROI proof.
FINANCE TAKEAWAY
Tier the tools. Fix the data. Measure the dollars saved, not the demos run.

SMB SUPPLY CHAIN · 2026 EDITION
METRIC 02 · THE 4-TIER STACK
Pick the cheapest tier that works.
 T0 · RULES
n8n, Make, Zapier. <$0.001 per run. Deterministic flows. Zero tokens.
T1 · OCR + RULES
Mindee, Document AI, Parseur. $0.01–0.05 per document. Structured invoices, POs, BOLs.
T2/T3 · SLM → LLM
GPT-4o-mini & Gemini Flash do 80% of tasks at 10–30× lower cost. Escalate to Claude/GPT-4 only when reasoning is
real.
FINANCE TAKEAWAY
Cascading from rules → SLM → LLM cuts inference cost 75–98%.

SMB SUPPLY CHAIN · 2026 EDITION
METRIC 03 · PURCHASE-TO-PAY WINS
Where AI pays for itself first.
 INVOICE CAPTURE
OCR + 3-way match. 87% automation rate. $8–15 → $1–3 per invoice. Payback in 3–6 months.
GL CODING & ROUTING
SLM + RAG on 12 months of history. 70–85% auto-coding accuracy. Approvals from days to hours.
SPEND ANALYSIS
Monthly batch SLM job classifies every line. Finds 3–8% tail-spend savings inside 90 days.
FINANCE TAKEAWAY
P2P is where the cash is. Start here — savings fund the rest of the program.

SMB SUPPLY CHAIN · 2026 EDITION
METRIC 04 · INTERNAL LOGISTICS WINS
Forecast, replenish, route.
 DEMAND FORECASTING
Prophet (open-source) at SKU-week level. Cuts stockouts 30–50%, excess stock 15–25%. Zero LLM tokens.
FREIGHT AUDIT
Cross-check every carrier invoice vs. contracted rates. Recovers 3–8% of freight spend.
ROUTE & PICK PATHS
Solver-based (Google OR-Tools, OptimoRoute). 10–20% fewer km, 15–35% picker productivity.
FINANCE TAKEAWAY
Logistics ROI comes from math and OCR. Use LLMs only to explain results to managers.

METRIC 05 · TOKEN ECONOMICS
Six levers that cut your AI bill in half.
 MODEL ROUTING
Send simple tasks to small models. FrugalGPT-style cascades: up to 98% cheaper.
STRUCTURED OUTPUT
Function calling + CSV beats free-form JSON. 30–60% fewer output tokens.
CACHE + BATCH
Semantic caching for repeat queries. Batch APIs at 50% discount overnight.
FINANCE TAKEAWAY
Architect for token efficiency on day one — it's the single biggest cost lever.
