FRACTIONAL COO FOR ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS

YOUR ELECTRICAL COMPANY SHOULDN’T STALL WHEN YOU’RE ON A JOB SITE.

Frontier helps electrical contractors doing $500K–$10M put bids, crews and scheduling on a system the team owns, so the business runs without you at the center.

Book a Discovery Call
Why does everything in your electrical company still run through you?

In electrical contracting, the owner is often still the license on the permits, the estimator on the bids and the scheduler for every crew. Growth adds crews faster than it adds people who can make decisions.

Bids wait on you to price them.
Crew leads call you before every change order.
Service and project work fight over the same people.
Inspections and permits slip because nobody owns the calendar.
New apprentices learn whatever the last guy did.

It’s not you. It’s how the business is designed. When nobody owns the outcome and nobody has the authority to decide, it all flows back to the owner.

How does Frontier get your electrical company off your back?

Frontier installs the operating system in four stages: Clarity → Alignment → Execution → Scale. For a electrical company, that means a clear owner for estimating, scheduling and permits, a weekly scorecard with the numbers that matter (bids out, win rate, crew utilization, change orders billed), a meeting rhythm your foremen actually run, and SOPs the crews use. Built ≠ Owned: it isn’t done until your team runs it without you.

Three ways to work together
Individual Coaching · $250/hr

One-on-one coaching for you or a key leader on planning, delegating, accountability and running the day-to-day.

System Builds · from $2,500

Electrical owner questions
What does a fractional COO do for a electrical company?

A fractional COO builds and runs the operating system a growing electrical company needs, part-time, without the cost of a full-time hire. That means a clear owner for every function, a weekly scorecard, a meeting rhythm, and SOPs, so day-to-day decisions stop landing on the owner.

How is that different from an electrical business coach or a software consultant?

A coach advises and a software consultant sets up tools. A fractional COO installs the system and stays until your team runs it. Frontier’s standard is Built ≠ Owned: nothing counts as done until your team uses it without you.

What does it cost?

Individual Coaching is $250 an hour. System Builds start at $2,500 per project. An Operating Partner engagement starts at $3,000 a month.

What size electrical company is this for?

Frontier works with founder-led electrical contractors doing $500K to $10M a year, especially where too many decisions still run through the owner.

Will this work with the software we already use?

Yes. Frontier builds the process around the tools you already run instead of selling you new software.

How long until the business runs without me?

It depends on where the business starts and which option you choose, so we won’t promise a date before we’ve looked. The Discovery Call is where you get an honest read.

Do you work with both service and commercial project electricians?

Yes. The operating system is the same; the scorecard, the handoffs and the roles change to fit the work.
