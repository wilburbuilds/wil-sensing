Individual Coaching
$250/hr

One-on-one coaching with an operator who has done the job.

BEST FOR:

Owners who want a sharper plan and a sounding board, or a key leader you are growing to run the day-to-day.

WHAT WE WORK ON:
Building a 90-day plan and actually executing it
Getting out of the day-to-day and delegating with confidence
Leading a team and holding people accountable
Making faster, clearer decisions under pressure
Running leadership meetings that drive the business
Having the hard conversations you have been putting off
Reading your basic financial documents
Growing from operator to leader, or from owner to CEO

System Builds
From $2,500

One defined project, built with your team and handed off working.

BEST FOR:

Owners who know what’s broken and want it fixed, installed and owned by the team.

PROJECTS INCLUDE:
A diagnosis of your biggest gaps, with a 30-, 60- or 90-day operational plan
An org chart that shows who owns what
A weekly scorecard with the few numbers that run the business
A weekly meeting rhythm that ends in decisions and clear to-dos
SOPs and checklists your team will actually use
A hiring and onboarding system that ramps new people fast
Clean handoffs between the office and the field

MOST COMPREHENSIVE
Operating Partner
From $3,000/mo

Billed monthly

I embed as your part-time COO and build the operating system with you, then run it alongside your team.

BEST FOR:

Owners ready to stop being responsible for everything, and leadership teams that need structure and accountability.

WHAT’S INCLUDED:
Diagnose how the business runs today and where it breaks
Build the org chart and define who owns what
Stand up the weekly scorecard and the numbers to watch
Set the weekly operating rhythm and run the meetings
Drive the priorities to done and hold the team accountable
Coach your leaders until they can run it without me
Step in when you are out of the office
