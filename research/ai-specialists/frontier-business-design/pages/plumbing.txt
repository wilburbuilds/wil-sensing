FRACTIONAL COO FOR PLUMBING OWNERS

YOUR PLUMBING COMPANY SHOULDN’T NEED YOU ON EVERY CALL.

Frontier helps plumbing owners doing $500K–$10M put emergency calls, pricing and techs on a system the team owns, so the business runs without you at the center.

Book a Discovery Call
Why does everything in your plumbing company still run through you?

Plumbing owners end up approving every price, sorting every emergency call and covering for every tech who doesn’t show. The work is good. The system behind it runs through one person.

Emergency calls get sorted by you, day and night.
Techs call you to price jobs on site.
Callbacks and warranty work aren’t tracked against anyone.
Dispatch and the CSRs work from different information.
You can’t tell which jobs actually make money.

It’s not you. It’s how the business is designed. When nobody owns the outcome and nobody has the authority to decide, it all flows back to the owner.

How does Frontier get your plumbing company off your back?

Frontier installs the operating system in four stages: Clarity → Alignment → Execution → Scale. For a plumbing company, that means a clear owner for dispatch, pricing and callbacks, a weekly scorecard with the numbers that matter (calls booked, average ticket, callbacks, margin by job type), a meeting rhythm your leads actually run, and SOPs the techs and CSRs use. Built ≠ Owned: it isn’t done until your team runs it without you.

Three ways to work together
Individual Coaching · $250/hr

One-on-one coaching for you or a key leader on planning, delegating, accountability and running the day-to-day.

System Builds · from $2,500

One defined project, like an org chart, a weekly scorecard, a dispatch or scheduling process, or a hiring system, built with your team and handed off working.

Operating Partner · from $3,000/mo

Frontier embeds as your part-time COO, builds the operating system with you and runs it alongside your team.

See everything included in each option.

Plumbing owner questions
What does a fractional COO do for a plumbing company?

A fractional COO builds and runs the operating system a growing plumbing company needs, part-time, without the cost of a full-time hire. That means a clear owner for every function, a weekly scorecard, a meeting rhythm, and SOPs, so day-to-day decisions stop landing on the owner.

How is that different from a plumbing business coach or a software consultant?

A coach advises and a software consultant sets up tools. A fractional COO installs the system and stays until your team runs it. Frontier’s standard is Built ≠ Owned: nothing counts as done until your team uses it without you.

What does it cost?

Individual Coaching is $250 an hour. System Builds start at $2,500 per project. An Operating Partner engagement starts at $3,000 a month.

What size plumbing company is this for?

Frontier works with founder-led plumbing owners doing $500K to $10M a year, especially where too many decisions still run through the owner.

Will this work with the software we already use?

Yes. Frontier builds the process around the tools you already run instead of selling you new software.

How long until the business runs without me?

It depends on where the business starts and which option you choose, so we won’t promise a date before we’ve looked. The Discovery Call is where you get an honest read.

Do you work with both service and new-construction plumbers?

Yes. The operating system is the same; the scorecard, the handoffs and the roles change to fit the work.
