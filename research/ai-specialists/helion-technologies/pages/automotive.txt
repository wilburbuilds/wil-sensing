IT and Cybersecurity for Automotive Dealerships

Helion Technologies helps automotive dealerships keep their stores running and customer data protected with managed IT, cybersecurity and DMS transition support. Request a Complimentary Assessment

Automotive dealerships rely on specialized software, vendor integrations and systems that have to stay available, so their IT calls for expertise in the DMS, cybersecurity and the protection of customer financial data.

The IT Risks Automotive Dealerships Face

Criminals know a dealership depends on constant system access to sell, finance and service cars, so a store that cannot run is more likely to pay a ransom to get going again.

Six pressures an automotive dealership has to plan for:

Customer financial data draws attackers.

Financial records, credit applications and other personal information make automotive dealerships frequent targets for ransomware and phishing.

Many attacks start in the inbox.

Deceptive emails can trick staff into granting access, and in business email compromise, criminals use or impersonate a trusted account to ask for money or data.

Microsoft 365 still needs the right controls.

Many dealerships prefer Microsoft 365, and on any email platform, multi-factor authentication, email filtering and access management stay essential.

An outage can disrupt sales and service.

When the DMS, CRM or phones go down, lead response, service scheduling and sales can suffer, and even a short outage can cost revenue.

Guest Wi-Fi has to stay separate.

A guest network for customers and visitors belongs apart from internal business networks, which helps keep outside devices away from systems that hold customer data.

Every integration is a potential way in.

CRM, service scheduling, inventory, accounting and OEM tools often connect to the DMS, and each third-party integration is another potential entry point.

The people supporting your store already know how a dealership runs, from the F&I office to the service drive.

Every Helion service, process and recommendation is built around the way dealerships operate.
