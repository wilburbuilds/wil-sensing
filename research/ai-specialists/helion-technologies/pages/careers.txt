Build Your Career at Helion

Benefits That Support You and Your Family

Helion’s benefits package covers your health, your savings and your time away from work, with coverage options for spouses and dependents.

Medical Coverage

Helion pays 100% of the individual employee premium for a high-deductible health plan with a nationwide provider network. Coverage starts on the 1st of the month after your hire date, and you can add eligible spouses and dependents.

Dental and Vision Insurance

Dental coverage supports preventive, basic and major care, and vision coverage includes routine eye exams, glasses and contact lenses. You choose the options that fit your family, with coverage for spouses and eligible children.

Healthcare Savings

A company-funded health reimbursement arrangement (HRA) helps offset eligible deductible expenses. A healthcare flexible spending account (FSA) covers qualified medical costs with pre-tax dollars, and a dependent care FSA helps with eligible childcare and dependent care.

Disability Coverage

Short-term disability coverage is available to help protect your income during a qualifying medical absence, and Helion pays 100% of the long-term disability coverage for eligible employees.

Life Insurance

Helion provides $25,000 of employer-paid life insurance for eligible employees. You can buy additional coverage for yourself, and supplemental options are available for spouses and dependent children.

Retirement Savings

Employees can join Helion’s 401(k) retirement savings plan, which comes with an employer match from Helion to help your savings grow, plus both traditional and long-term planning options.

Paid Time Off

You receive three weeks of paid time off in your first year, and PTO increases with tenure. Use it for vacation, personal time or illness, with paid holidays throughout the year as well.
