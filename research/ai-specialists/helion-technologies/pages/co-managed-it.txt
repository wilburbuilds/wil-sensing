Co-Managed IT for Dealerships

Co-Managed IT for Dealerships, Built to Support Your Team, Not Replace It

Helion's team works alongside your existing IT staff, adding coverage where it's stretched thin.

Get Your Complimentary IT & Cybersecurity Assessment →

Co-managed IT for dealerships means an internal IT person or team keeps their role while a managed provider adds coverage, expertise, and backup around them. Helion Technologies offers this alongside full-service management, so a dealership isn't forced to choose between an internal hire and outside support.

 One internal IT hire, all at once
That's a lot for anyone to carry alone.

One internal IT hire, or even a small internal team, is being asked to cover network administration, cybersecurity, compliance, DMS support, and day-to-day helpdesk requests all at once.

Especially when a single dealership issue, like the DMS going down on a Saturday sales event, can't wait for someone to get back from vacation or catch up after being out sick.

The usual assumption is that fixing this means bringing in an outside provider to take over entirely, which understandably worries the person already doing the job. That's not the only option.

Full Service

A real path for dealerships that want complete outside management.

Co-Managed

A different one, built for dealerships that already have IT staff worth keeping in place.

What Co-Managed Actually Means

Co-managed IT means Helion's team works alongside an existing internal IT person or department instead of replacing them.

The exact division of responsibilities gets worked out based on what a dealership's internal team already handles well and where the gaps actually are, not a fixed template applied the same way everywhere. Strategy and budget planning are a common piece to hand over, and our vCIO for dealerships page covers that work.

Co-managed tends to work best where the internal IT director wants exactly this arrangement.

A dealership doesn't need to have this figured out on day one either. Circumstances change, and sometimes that shift is the moment a dealership decides to move from co-managed to full service. That path stays open in either direction.

"Will This Replace My IT Person?"

No.

That's the point of this model specifically. An internal IT person doesn't get pushed out or sidelined. They get a team behind them instead of standing alone against everything that can go wrong on a given day.

“
"Why not just hire another internal person instead?"

Hiring adds one more person covering the same narrow set of specialties one person already knows. Co-managed adds a full team's worth of expertise across networking, cybersecurity, compliance, and dealership systems, without the cost or timeline of building that out internally from scratch. Our in-house vs managed vs co-managed IT comparison puts all three side by side.

“
"Will this create confusion about who handles what?"

That's exactly what gets worked out before anything starts. A dealership's internal team and Helion's team don't operate on guesswork about who owns which part of the stack. Our guide to every system in a dealership technology stack and who usually supports it is a good place to start that list. Then use our co-managed IT responsibility matrix to put one owner on every row.
