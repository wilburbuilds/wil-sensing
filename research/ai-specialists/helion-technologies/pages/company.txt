ABOUT HELION TECHNOLOGIES

Purpose-Built for Automotive and Heavy-Truck Dealerships

Helion Technologies is a managed IT and cybersecurity company that works only with automotive and heavy-truck dealerships. Founded in 1997 by Erik Nachbahr and based in Baltimore, Maryland, Helion supports more than 2,000 dealerships and 35,000 end users from one integrated team.

HOW HELION WORKS

Technology That Moves Your Dealership Forward

Technology should give a dealership an edge, not another operating problem. Helion brings managed IT, cybersecurity, Tekion enablement, DMS transition support and managed AI together in one program built only for automotive and heavy-truck dealerships. FTC Safeguards Rule support is part of Helion’s managed services for its clients.

Helion keeps improving each dealership’s technology instead of reacting after something breaks, so the people running the store can focus on growing the business.

What Helion Works Toward
Better performance from the dealership’s technology.
Lower cyber risk for the dealership.
One accountable partner instead of several vendors to coordinate.
Technology that supports the dealership’s growth.
