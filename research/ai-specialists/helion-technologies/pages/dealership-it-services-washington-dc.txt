One Metro, Three Sets of Privacy Rules

The FTC Safeguards Rule covers every dealer that arranges financing or leasing, on either side of the Potomac. That part never moves. What changes at the state line is the privacy law stacked on top of it, and around Washington a single dealer group can easily own stores under all three of the rows below.

JURISDICTION	COMPREHENSIVE PRIVACY LAW	IN FORCE	AT THE STORE
District of Columbia	
No comprehensive consumer privacy law
No state law	None	The FTC Safeguards Rule is the standard to meet.
Maryland	
Maryland Online Data Privacy Act (MODPA)
Dealership exempt	October 1, 2025	Financial institutions under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act are exempt as whole entities.
Virginia	
Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)
Dealership exempt	January 1, 2023	The same entity-level exemption, written two years earlier.

So is a Maryland or Virginia dealer off the hook? Not really. The exemption exists because the dealership already answers to the federal rule, which asks for a written security program, a named qualified individual, multi-factor authentication, encryption, testing and an incident response plan. An exemption from the state law is not an exemption from doing the work, and a lawyer should confirm how it applies to any related companies in the group that don’t arrange financing.

Maryland’s law is the newer of the two. We covered it when it took effect in why Maryland’s data privacy law matters for dealers everywhere. Our dealership compliance team keeps one written program that holds up in all three places. The primary sources are short reads, too. Try the FTC’s Safeguards Rule FAQ for auto dealers and the Maryland Attorney General’s data privacy page.

Managed IT Services for Washington, DC Dealers, or Help for the IT Person You Have

Most stores around here land in one of two camps. Some have no IT staff at all and want one team to own everything. Others have a capable IT manager who is buried. Either works.

For the first camp, managed IT for dealerships puts the help desk, monitoring, patching, backups and field dispatch under one monthly number with no surprise invoices, so nobody in the service lane has to stop and wonder whether a call to us is going to cost extra. For the second, co-managed IT leaves your person in charge and puts a dealership-only bench behind them. Vacations get covered. So do the 2 a.m. alerts.

Whenever your Washington, DC stores are open, 7 days a week, the help desk is open too. That includes Saturday, the busiest sales day of the week.
