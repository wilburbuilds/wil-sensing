DMS transition support helps dealerships migrate between dealer management systems while protecting data, minimizing downtime, and closing the security gaps that open up during a switch. Helion Technologies supports the full transition process for automotive and heavy truck dealerships, not just the final cutover.

A DMS switch creates a window where a dealership is more exposed than usual.

"Can't We Just Handle This With Our Existing IT Support?"

Sometimes.

The risk isn't a lack of general IT capability, it's the specific, temporary vulnerabilities a transition creates that don't exist on a normal day. Misconfigured integrations and incomplete backups are the kind of mistakes that are easy to make once and expensive to fix after the fact. If the DMS goes down mid-cutover, our DMS outage plan guide covers how each department keeps working on paper.

"How far in advance should we start planning?"

Earlier than most dealerships assume. Backups, permission audits, integration documentation, and a real timeline all need to happen before the switch, not during it. Heavy truck stores still picking a platform can start with our Karmak vs Procede comparison. Stores already on Fusion can see our Karmak Fusion IT support. Car stores can start with our DMS conversion checklist, and those still choosing a platform can line up CDK, Reynolds, Tekion and Dealertrack in our DMS comparison. Choosing between Tekion and CDK? Read our Tekion vs CDK comparison from the IT side. On Reynolds? Our Tekion vs Reynolds and Reynolds comparison asks the same questions, server room included.
