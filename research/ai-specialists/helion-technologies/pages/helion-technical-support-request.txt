Helion Technical Support Request

Helion Technologies’ support team helps staff at Helion client dealerships with technical problems, from a locked account to a DMS workstation that won’t connect. The support request form on this page sends the details of your problem straight to that team.

No Client Portal sign-in? Use the support request form below.

Open the Client Portal
What to Put in the Description

Use the issue title for the short version, like “Parts counter printer offline,” and the description for the rest. Write it the way you’d explain it to a technician standing at your desk.

Where it’s happening. The store, the department and the computer, printer or system involved.
What happened. What you were doing when it started, plus any error message copied word for word.
Who it affects. Say how many people are stuck. One person locked out and a whole service drive that can’t reach the DMS are very different problems.
What you’ve tried. Restarting, signing out and back in, or anything else you’ve already done.
