Every dealership ends up in the same place. A DMS provider. A separate network vendor. A phone system vendor. Maybe a compliance vendor bolted on after the last audit scare. Nobody owns the whole system, and nothing about it is strategic — it's just what accumulated.

It doesn't matter if that's one overworked internal IT hire or a full department spread too thin. When a breach hits, an FTC Safeguards audit lands, or a Tekion migration goes sideways, a patchwork of vendors means nobody's actually accountable for the outcome.

Helion Technologies is a managed IT, cybersecurity, compliance, and Tekion Certified DMS Enablement provider built exclusively for automotive and heavy truck dealerships. One team responsible for the whole stack, not four vendors pointing at each other. Founded in 1997, Helion now serves over 2,000 dealerships nationwide.
