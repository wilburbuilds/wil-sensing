IT SUPPORT FOR DEALERSHIPS

Managed IT for Dealerships, Built to Keep Sales and Service Running

Managed IT for dealerships means outsourcing network monitoring, helpdesk support, and DMS uptime to a provider that understands dealership-specific systems. Helion Technologies delivers this exclusively for automotive and heavy truck dealerships. Helion built its entire model around dealership IT specifically, not IT in general.

01
Proactive Monitoring

Systems get watched before they fail, not after. But the deeper differentiator is what’s behind that monitoring: nearly 30 years of watching how dealership technology actually holds up over time, across 35,000 dealership employees supported today.

That history gets overlaid onto every assessment, which is why Helion can walk into a new dealership and say, this is the vetted firewall, this is the vetted wireless setup, this is the vetted PC configuration, deployed tens of thousands of times across the client base already.
