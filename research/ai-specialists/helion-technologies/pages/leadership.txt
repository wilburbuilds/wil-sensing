Helion Technologies Leadership

Founder and president Erik Nachbahr, a CISSP, leads Helion Technologies, which he founded in 1997. Based in Baltimore, Maryland, Helion provides managed IT, cybersecurity, Tekion enablement, DMS transition support and managed AI only to automotive and heavy-truck dealerships.

Leadership

The Founder Behind Helion
Erik Nachbahr, CISSP

Founder & President, Helion Technologies

Erik Nachbahr founded Helion Technologies in 1997 and has spent nearly 30 years working exclusively with automotive and heavy truck dealerships. Helion now supports more than 2,000 dealerships and 35,000 end users with managed IT, cybersecurity, Tekion enablement, and managed AI, all under one team. A CISSP, Erik writes and speaks about the parts of dealership technology most operators inherit rather than choose: vendor sprawl, AI governance, and cyber risk. His goal is simple: make the technology work so dealerships can focus on what they do best, selling and servicing cars and heavy trucks.
