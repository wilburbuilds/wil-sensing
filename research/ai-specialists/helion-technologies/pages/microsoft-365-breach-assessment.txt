Most cybercriminals don’t force their way in. They log in quietly, blend in and start gathering information.

Is Someone Already Inside Your Microsoft 365?

Once inside, an attacker can monitor email, explore files, search for sensitive data and look for wider access, all while appearing to be a legitimate user. A license alone doesn’t catch that, as we explain in what Microsoft 365 Business Premium security covers and what it leaves out. After the assessment, managed Microsoft 365 for dealerships is the ongoing service that runs the tenant every day.

Helion Technologies has worked only with automotive and heavy-truck dealerships for nearly 30 years. Founded in 1997 and based in Baltimore, Maryland, it supports more than 2,000 dealerships and 35,000 end users, and its cybersecurity specialists carry out this assessment.

What the Assessment Provides
 Security posture. An evaluation of how your Microsoft 365 environment is secured.
 Indicators of compromise. A hunt for signs that someone is already inside.
 Configuration weaknesses. The settings that could leave the environment exposed.
 Practical recommendations. Steps to strengthen your defenses.
