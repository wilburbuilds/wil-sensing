Helion Technologies runs Managed IT, Cybersecurity, Compliance, Tekion Enablement, and Managed AI as one sequenced, accountable program for dealerships, rather than five separate vendors each covering their own piece.

Technology doesn't operate in silos. Neither should the team managing it.

Most dealerships end up with a DMS provider, a network vendor, a phone system vendor, and a compliance vendor bolted on somewhere along the way. Each does its own job. None of them talk to each other. When something breaks, no single vendor actually owns the outcome. Our map of the dealership technology stack shows who usually supports each system and where the gaps sit.

Helion brings Managed IT, Cybersecurity, Compliance, Tekion Enablement, and Managed AI together in a single, integrated program built exclusively for automotive and heavy truck dealerships, each pillar building on the one before it, so nothing gets handled in isolation and nothing falls through the gap between vendors.

CONTINUOUS COMPLIANCE MANAGEMENT

Most compliance programs hand a dealership a checklist once a year and call it done.

Helion's Managed Compliance Program works differently: technical evidence gets collected directly from a dealership's own systems, validated, and coordinated straight into remediation through the same Managed IT and Cybersecurity teams, not documented and handed back for someone else to fix.

Continuous Evidence Collection

Gathered directly from the technology environment, not manually assembled from spreadsheets and screenshots.

Active Risk Remediation

Vulnerabilities and gaps become operational work immediately, not another item on a list.

One Integrated Team

Compliance specialists, cybersecurity professionals, and Managed IT engineers working from the same operational data.

Compliance isn't a portal. It's an operational program backed by technology, cybersecurity, and people working together.
