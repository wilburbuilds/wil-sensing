How We Find Elite Talent For You

Learn more about the process we use to judge hundreds of applications every single day. The following steps are the key to providing you with the best talent.

Attract Top Performers

At Hello Paralegal, we source exceptional legal talent from our own brand, LawSikho, and Market. We've cultivated Hello Paralegal into an employer of choice by offering competitive pay, great benefits, and a strong company culture. We actively seek out the best legal professionals to ensure you receive top-tier talent.

Ensure The Right Fit For Your Role

We screen hundreds of resumes each day to identify the few paralegals that best match the requirements of our clients' open roles. To pass this stage, a candidate must possess all the required technical skills and sufficient relevant experience.

First Interview

Once a candidate passes the initial screening, we invite them to an online interview to showcase their work experience, communication skills, and cultural fit. During this interview, we assess their overall alignment with Hello Paralegal's values and your team's needs.

Skills Test

For most roles, we require candidates to complete a test to prove they truly have the required expertise. The tests vary in length and complexity but are an excellent way to identify the best paralegals.

Final Interview With Senior Leadership

The final step is an interview with our leadership team. Here, we double-check that the paralegal has all the required skills to support their designated client, ensure cultural fit, and look for any red flags.

Finalisation, Payment Discussions & Onboarding

If the provided candidate and fees are accepted on your end, we finalise the agreement, discuss payment terms, and begin the onboarding process. Our team ensures a smooth transition for your paralegal, aligning with your expectations and workflow.

Ongoing Support

Once your paralegal is placed, our team continues to provide ongoing support to ensure everything runs smoothly and efficiently
