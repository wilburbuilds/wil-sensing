Nick Saraev — founder of LeftClick.

AI entrepreneur, educator, and builder. 400K+ on YouTube, #1 AI community on Skool, and $10M+ generated for clients.

Nick Saraev founded LeftClick to do one thing: build AI systems that actually move revenue for B2B companies. He was early — in 2019, well before ChatGPT, he was working with GPT-2 and other early models. One of his projects, 1SecondPainting, went viral on Hacker News.

From there he founded 1SecondCopy, an AI content agency he scaled to $90K/mo with a single partner — proving the financial utility of generative AI years before it was obvious. He went on to launch and scale LeftClick, and to build Maker School into the #1 AI community on Skool, with over $330,000/mo in revenue and 10,000+ graduates.

Today Nick runs a YouTube channel with 400K+ subscribers, has created Claude Code courses with 20M+ views across YouTube and X, and has generated over $10M in revenue for clients across 20+ industries. Everything LeftClick sells has been demonstrated, explained, and stress-tested in public — hundreds of thousands of times over.
