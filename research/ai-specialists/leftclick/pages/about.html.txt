Building with AI since 2019.

In 2019, Nick started working with GPT-2 and other early models to do bulk image generation. He posted a few personal projects to Hacker News, and one of them (1SecondPainting) went viral.

From there, he began experimenting with GPT-3 to automate content production, and eventually founded 1SecondCopy, an AI content agency. He scaled that to $90K/mo with a partner, proving the financial utility of generative AI.

Later, he'd launch and scale LeftClick to $72K/mo, build Maker School into the #1 AI community on Skool (over $330,000/mo in revenue), and personally help 10,000+ people graduate from the program.

He's since launched a YouTube channel that has grown to over 400K subscribers, created Claude Code courses with over 20M+ views across YouTube and X, and generated over $10M in revenue for clients across 20+ industries.
