Work with us.

We hire exceptional, senior engineers and consultants who want to build AI that ships.

LeftClick is a small, senior team. We don't have layers of account managers between the people who sell the work and the people who build it — everyone here is hands-on, and everyone owns outcomes.

We work fixed-price and in the open: real scopes, working demos, weekly updates. If you're the kind of person who'd rather ship a system that runs reliably than write a deck about one, you'll like it here.

Who we look for
Builders, not advisers

You've shipped production systems and you can own a project end to end, from scoping through deployment.

Senior AI & automation skill

Deep experience with LLMs, automation platforms, APIs, and the glue that makes them reliable in production.

Clear communicators

You can explain a technical tradeoff to a founder and a board, and write documentation people actually use.

High standards

You care that what we promise in the boardroom actually ships — tested, documented, and built to last.
