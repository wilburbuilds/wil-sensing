We helped 1SecondCopy automate 90% of their pipeline—from lead gen to delivery—turning a thin-margin content agency into a high-leverage, scalable business with fewer than 3 operators.

AI Cold Email Systems
Application Systems
Content Ops Systems
1SecondCopy
AI-Augmented Content Agency, launched 2021
Outcomes
We automated ~90% of the lead-to-delivery pipeline, removing human touch from most operations.
We cut cost per 1,000 words by 50-60% versus traditional agencies while maintaining healthy margins.
We scaled the business from zero to hundreds of clients with <3 internal operators.
We made same-day turnaround the norm and held the revision rate below 5%.
We eliminated 40+ hrs/month of admin time for billing and payroll.
Challenge

1SecondCopy was competing on price in a commoditizing content market. Market rates for copywriting far exceeded the cost of AI-assisted production, but they had no infrastructure to capture that margin at scale.

Solution
We built custom bots that bid on freelance-platform jobs with GPT-generated cover letters and dynamic pricing logic.
We deployed a job-board scraper that identified decision-makers and fed them into a personalized cold-email engine.
We created a self-serve client portal that auto-generated Drive folders/docs and routed tasks to writers via an AI-driven project board.
We designed a multi-stage hiring funnel (LinkedIn, Indeed, Reddit, FB) that vetted, trial-tested, and auto-onboarded contractors with AI training packs.
We wired time-tracking and cost dashboards into automated semi-monthly payroll (invoices + Wise drafts).
