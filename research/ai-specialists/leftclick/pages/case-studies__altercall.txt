By marrying granular engagement tracking with multi-channel automation and live sales analytics, we turned Alter Call's pricey leads into consistently high value clients—unlocking predictable growth in a hyper-competitive market.

Multi-Channel Nurture Systems
Engagement Scoring Systems
Sales-Ops Automation
AlterCall
High-ticket personal-development programs by influencer Ryan Blair
Challenge

Facebook ads brought leads at a premium CPL; every contact had to be nurtured with just right pressure to maximize conversions without alienating prospects.

Solution
Built behavior-driven nurture engine spanning SMS, email, Facebook DM/group invites and scheduled calls.
Engagement signals (course-progress %, email opens, asset downloads, group joins) fed a lead-score that dictated cadence and channel.
Sales-ops layer: setter/closer scoreboard, tiered-commission logic, and call-outcome forms auto-enforced via SMS nudges to managers.
Unified dashboard tracked dials, show-ups, close-rate, cash-collected and product split in real time.
Impact
Call-booking rate up ~30%.
Close-rate per booked call up ~25%.
ROAS improved by ~40% as high-ticket sales offset ad costs.
Transparent performance data fostered healthy competition and accelerated promotion of top setters to closers.
