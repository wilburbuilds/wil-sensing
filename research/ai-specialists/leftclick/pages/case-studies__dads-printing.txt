Dad's Printing
Custom-merch producer pivoting from wholesale to direct-to-consumer
Challenge

Low-margin B2B orders; production not built for high-mix, high-volume personalization.

Solution
Launched three Etsy stores aimed at high-demand niches (custom pet socks, face masks, etc.).
Photoshop scripting auto-generated thousands of product mock-ups.
Pipeline: Etsy → PM board → offshore design team → API-driven print-sheet nesting with embedded order # & barcode.
Barcode scan triggered one-touch print of label, manifest (box-size logic), and invoice; final scan closed order and pushed tracking to customer.
Production floor split by printer role for flow-through efficiency.
Impact
#1 ranking in every targeted Etsy Canada category during peak Christmas season; dominated first-page results across all three stores.
Margin per unit up 2–3× versus wholesale jobs.
Hundreds of unique orders/day with <5% error rate.
Pick-pack time cut ~70%; shipping cost optimized by auto-box selection.
DTC now delivers the majority of gross profit while wholesale continues uninterrupted.
