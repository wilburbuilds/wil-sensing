What is LeftClick?

LeftClick (also known as LeftClick AI) is the AI growth partner for fast-moving B2B companies. Here's exactly who we are and what we do.

LeftClick is an AI automation and growth consultancy for B2B companies. We build custom AI systems that generate leads, close deals, and scale operations — without our clients having to add headcount. LeftClick was founded by Nick Saraev in 2022, and our team has seven years of production AI experience across more than 20 industries. To date we've generated over $10M in revenue for the companies we work with.

We don't just advise — we build, deploy, and manage the systems. Every engagement is fixed-price with a clear scope and timeline, and we use the same automations we sell to run our own companies, so they're battle-tested before they reach a client.

Not to be confused with

“LeftClick” is a common term, and there are other companies and tools that share the name. This site — leftclick.ai — is LeftClick AI, the B2B AI growth consultancy founded by Nick Saraev. If you were looking for our AI services, you're in the right place.
