Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026

Mad City Integrations LLC (“Mad City Integrations,” “MCI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit madcityintegrations.com, submit an inquiry, book a consultation, communicate with us, or use our services.

By using our website or providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

Your name
Business name
Email address
Telephone number
Services you are interested in
The reason for your inquiry
Notes or other information you choose to provide
Consultation scheduling information
Communications you send to us
Your consent preferences for calls and text messages

We may also collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as browser type, device type, IP address, request logs, timestamps, and referring pages. We do not currently use advertising pixels or behavioral advertising tools.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, protected health information, financial account information, passwords, government identification numbers, or confidential client data through the public consultation form.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

Respond to inquiries
Evaluate how our services may help a business
Schedule and conduct consultations
Provide requested follow-up information
Send consultation confirmations and scheduling reminders
Communicate about services a person has specifically requested
Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the website
Improve our forms, services, and customer experience
Maintain business and compliance records
Prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents
Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We will not use an optional phone number for non-marketing calls or text messages through the consultation workflow unless the person affirmatively provides the applicable consent or otherwise directly initiates the communication.

3. Calls and Text Messages

When you affirmatively check the calls and text messages consent box, you agree that Mad City Integrations LLC may contact you at the number you provide regarding your inquiry, consultation scheduling, and requested follow-up.

These communications are non-marketing in nature and may include:

Acknowledgment of your inquiry
Consultation booking information
Booking confirmations
Scheduling reminders
A reminder to complete consultation booking
Responses related to your specific request

Messages may be sent using automated technology. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request assistance by replying HELP or contacting contact@madcityintegrations.com.

Opting out of text messages does not prevent us from responding by email or from making communications that are legally required or otherwise permitted.

4. How We Share Information

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate our website and business, such as:

Website hosting and deployment providers, including Vercel
Customer relationship management and communications providers, including GoHighLevel
Google Workspace and Google Calendar
Email, scheduling, infrastructure, security, and form-processing providers
Professional advisers, such as attorneys, accountants, or insurers
Government agencies or other parties when disclosure is required by law

These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us or as otherwise permitted by their agreements and applicable law.

We may also disclose information:

To comply with a legal obligation, subpoena, court order, or lawful government request
To investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse
To enforce agreements or protect our legal rights
To protect the safety and rights of MCI, our clients, website users, or others
In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction
5. Mobile Information and SMS Consent

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile telephone numbers, text-message opt-in data, or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share mobile information with vendors that support delivery of our communications, scheduling, customer relationship management, hosting, security, or related business operations. Those providers may use the information only to perform services for us and not for their own independent marketing.

6. Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We do not currently share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use essential cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for functionality, security, session management, and reliable operation.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioral analytics tools. If our practices materially change, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement any consent mechanism required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

Respond to an inquiry
Maintain an active business relationship
Provide services
Maintain business and compliance records
Resolve disputes
Enforce agreements
Meet legal, accounting, tax, and security obligations

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer reasonably needed.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information.

However, no website, transmission method, cloud platform, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You provide information through the website at your own risk.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to:

Ask what personal information we maintain about you
Request correction of inaccurate information
Request deletion of information, subject to legal and operational exceptions
Withdraw communication consent
Ask questions about our privacy practices

Submit requests to contact@madcityintegrations.com.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We may retain information when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, contractual obligations, or legitimate business recordkeeping.

11. State Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence and whether applicable legal thresholds are met, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, portability, or certain uses of personal information.

You may submit a request through contact@madcityintegrations.com. We will evaluate and respond to valid requests as required by applicable law.

Because we do not currently sell personal information or use personal information for targeted advertising, we do not currently provide a separate sale or targeted-advertising opt-out mechanism.

12. Children's Privacy

The website and our services are intended for adults and authorized business representatives who are at least 18 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

13. Third-Party Websites and Services

The website may link to or integrate with third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy, security, accuracy, availability, or practices of third-party websites or services.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make changes, we will post the updated policy and revise the effective date.

Material changes may also be communicated through the website or another reasonable method.
