Claim Your Seat
PHASE 01
Discover and Scope

Find the problem worth solving and turn it into work someone will pay for.

PILLAR 1
Process Audit & AI Fit

Run discovery calls that uncover the real problem (and reveal the solution)

What you’ll learn
Reframe a client’s stated request to uncover the problem underneath it
Map how the work actually flows today, including the parts nobody documents
Score each opportunity on the value it creates and how much AI can really do
Sort everything into one prioritized list: do it now, do it later, or leave it alone
PILLAR 2
Engagement Design

Scope, price, and protect the work so you don’t get burned

What you’ll learn
Define the outcome the client is buying, stated in their numbers rather than your activities
Draw a scope line with a clear “done” test on everything inside it
Price the work against the value of the outcome instead of the hours it takes
Set the terms that govern payment and protect the scope when requests start creeping

YOUR OUTPUT
A signed statement of work.

PHASE 02
Architect and Build

The design decisions that separate a demo from a system that runs live.

PILLAR 3
Context, Workflow & Reliability

Build systems that don’t break the moment a real user touches them

What you’ll learn
Engineer the context a model needs, and diagnose the ways context degrades
Draw the code-versus-model line so the right component makes each decision
Map real-world scenarios and build in milestones instead of discovering gaps in production
Apply the reliability practices that make a build something a client can actually depend on
PILLAR 4
AI Governance

Answer the questions legal, compliance, and security teams will ask

What you’ll learn
Decide what an AI system is permitted to do, touch, and act on
Set data handling boundaries appropriate to the client’s risk and industry
Define escalation paths and ownership for when the system gets something wrong
Build those boundaries into the system as design requirements, not paperwork after the fact
PHASE 03
Evaluate and Iterate

Prove the system works in measurable terms, and keep proving it after launch.

PILLAR 5
AI Evals

Prove your systems work and catch performance drift before the client does

What you’ll learn
Define what “working” means for a specific build, in terms you can measure
Build evaluation methods across deterministic checks, model-graded review, and human review
Set up the feedback loop that catches drift before the client does
Use eval results to decide what to fix, what to accept, and when the work is genuinely done
PILLAR 6
AI Economics

Model costs, measure ROI, and justify the spend to CFOs

What you’ll learn
Model what a workflow costs to run against the cost of the work it replaces
Choose models and configurations against cost and performance tradeoffs
Measure and report the impact a deployed system actually produced
Build the business case that gets a finance-minded decision maker to yes
PHASE 04
Deliver and Sustain

Deliver the system so the team adopts it, and keep it running.

PILLAR 7
Adoption & Capability Building

Train teams, earn trust, and track whether your systems are actually used

What you’ll learn
Plan a delivery designed around adoption rather than bolted on at the end
Produce the training, documentation, and enablement materials a team needs to run the system
Handle the trust, change management, and workflow disruption that stall real rollouts
Track usage and report on whether the system is actually being used and holding up
