Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Web Intelligence, a tool by Pixel Narratives, handles the information submitted through this service. We are committed to being transparent about what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can have it removed.

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

When you submit a URL for auditing, we collect and store the following:

The URL you submit for analysis
The business type you select (e.g. restaurant, e-commerce)
The full audit results produced for that URL, including scores, page metadata, and AI-generated recommendations
Publicly available business profile data retrieved for the audited site, including Google rating, review count, and related authority signals
The timestamp of when the audit was run

We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or any other personally identifiable information. No account is required to use this tool.

HOW WE USE IT

The data we store is used solely to:

Display your audit history on the Dashboard
Enable the Compare feature across multiple audits
Power anonymised benchmarks that help contextualize scores by business type

We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising or marketing purposes.

DATA RETENTION

Audit records are retained indefinitely so that history and comparison features remain functional. If you wish to have a specific audit or all audits associated with a URL removed, see the data deletion section below.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

This tool relies on three external services to produce audit results:

Google PageSpeed Insights API — Performance, accessibility, and SEO scores are fetched from Google's public API. The URL you submit is sent to Google to generate these scores and is subject to Google's terms and data policies.
Google Places API — The submitted URL's domain is used to look up publicly available Google Business Profile data (rating, review count, listing status). This lookup is subject to Google Maps Platform Terms of Service.
AI Analysis (OpenAI) — Crawled page content and scores are sent to an AI model to generate written recommendations and summaries. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

We do not control how these third-party providers handle data once it leaves our system. Please review their respective privacy policies for details.

AUTOMATED CRAWLING

When you submit a URL, our automated crawler (SiteAuditBot/1.0) sends HTTP requests directly to the target site to gather page content, links, and metadata. By submitting a URL, you confirm you own or have explicit permission to audit that website. Do not submit URLs for sites you are not authorised to analyse.

DATA DELETION

You can delete individual audits from the Recent Audits list on the home page or from the Dashboard. If you would like all records for a particular URL removed from our database, or have any other data-related requests, please contact us at pixelnarratives.studio/contact and we will process your request promptly.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as the tool evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date at the top of this page.

ACCURACY

Results are based on a sample of up to 20 pages and may not reflect the full site. Findings are AI-generated and may vary between runs on the same site.

AUTHORIZED USE

By submitting a URL, you confirm you own or have explicit permission to audit that website. Do not use this tool on sites you are not authorized to analyze.

DATA STORAGE

Submitted URLs, business type, and audit results are stored to power benchmarking and history features.

PERFORMANCE DATA
