III / QUESTIONS

HOW DOES AN ENGAGEMENT START?

With a free fifteen-minute call. We map where AI actually helps you and where it will not, then scope the work on the spot if we are a fit.

WHAT DOES IT COST?

Enterprise engagements run from $15k to $50k and up, depending on scope. Advisory is a monthly retainer. You will know the number before any work begins.

DO YOU WORK WITH NON-TECHNICAL TEAMS?

Constantly. Most of the people we train have never written code. The point is to make Claude Code and Codex usable by the people who own the work.

WHICH TOOLS DO YOU BUILD ON?

Primarily Claude Code and Codex, wired into the systems you already run. We are tool-agnostic where it matters and opinionated where it counts.

HOW FAST CAN WE START?

Discovery this week, scoping the next. Implementation timelines depend on the state of your back of house, which is the first thing we audit.
