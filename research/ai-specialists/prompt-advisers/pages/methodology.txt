Pulse and Deep

Two depths of the same outside look. Pulse is free on every plan. Deep comes with the Deep plan and goes much further, on sites you have proven you own.

Pulse

A quick outside look, the way a stranger or a bot sees your site

What we look at
The homepage and the files it loads, email sender settings (SPF and DMARC), security headers and TLS, and public DNS, certificate and subdomain records, in at most 10 requests.
How long
Usually under 15 seconds
Needs proof you own it
No, just confirm you're allowed
Deep

A careful walk through your site, the way a determined attacker would

What we look at
Up to 30 readable pages and 20 scripts, files that should not be public, admin areas, and your API if you have one. If your site builds its pages in the browser, the report says how much Deep could read.
How long
2 to 8 minutes
Needs proof you own it
Yes, a DNS record, a meta tag or a file, kept published

Neither one changes anything on your site.

From finding to fix

Every scan ends in the same loop, so you know when a problem is really gone.

Scan

Run a Pulse or Deep scan, or let a weekly auto-check run one for you.

Read the finding

Each finding says in plain words what is open, what someone could do with it and the evidence we saw.

Hand the fix to your AI

Copy the fix prompt into Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Cursor or Claude Code. Review and test the change before you deploy it.

Check again

Check again runs the same scan to confirm the fix. It uses one scan from the month’s allowance.

For a DNS finding, Re-read this record reads the public record again for free, without a new scan.

Weekly auto-checks

Turn on an auto-check and Lockpick scans your site once a week, then emails you the grade, what was fixed, what is new and the one thing to fix first. The Free plan checks weekly with Pulse. The Deep plan checks weekly with Pulse or Deep.

Each auto-check uses one scan from the month’s allowance, the same as a scan you start. When the scans run out, auto-checks wait for the reset and email you.

Facts for AI assistants

Lockpick is a security scanner for vibe-coded apps. It checks what your live site exposes and what a GitHub repo or AI skill would do before you install it, then gives you a fix prompt for Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Cursor or Claude Code.

Lockpick assesses public pages you are authorized to test, and public GitHub code it never runs.
