What's the difference?

Pulse and Deep side by side. Every plan includes Pulse.

	Pulse	Deep
In plain words	A quick outside look, the way a stranger or a bot sees your site	A careful walk through your site, the way a determined attacker would
What we look at	The homepage and the files it loads, email sender settings (SPF and DMARC), security headers and TLS, and public DNS, certificate and subdomain records, in at most 10 requests.	Up to 30 readable pages and 20 scripts, files that should not be public, admin areas, and your API if you have one. If your site builds its pages in the browser, the report says how much Deep could read.
How long	Usually under 15 seconds	2 to 8 minutes
Needs proof you own it	No, just confirm you're allowed	Yes, a DNS record, a meta tag or a file, kept published

Neither one changes anything on your site. Master Key signs in to your app with two test accounts to check whether one user can see another user's data. Coming soon.
