 Frequently Asked Questions

 You have questions, we have answers

 I've been burned by an agency before. How are you different?

 You're not alone — many businesses come to us after bad agency experiences. Long contracts, vague reports, and no real clarity.

 We built our process to be the opposite. You own everything we create — your website, Google Business Profile, content, and citations. No transfer fees, no hostage situations.

 We don’t lock you into contracts. You stay because it’s working. And everything we do is documented so you always know what’s happening and why.

 Do I have to sign a contract?

 No. We work month to month. If we're not delivering, you can walk away.

 Some clients choose a quarterly commitment for a discounted rate, but that's optional. We’d rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

 What if it doesn't work?

 Then you leave — that’s the benefit of no contracts.

 From day one, you’ll have full tracking set up: call tracking, analytics, search data, and rankings. You’ll see exactly what’s happening.

 We also have regular check-ins, and if we ever feel we’re not the right fit, we’ll tell you. We’d rather be honest than keep a client we can’t help.

 Do I own my website and Google Business Profile?

 Yes — 100%. Everything we build or optimize belongs to you.

 If we part ways, you keep your website, Google Business Profile, content, and all assets. No fees, no strings, no negotiations.

 What does it cost to work with you?

 Most clients invest between $2,500 and $5,000 per month depending on market, competition, and goals.

 We’ll review your situation on a call and recommend the right plan based on where you are and where you want to go.

 How long until I see results?

 Weeks 1–4: Foundation work — setup, optimization, and tracking.

 Months 2–3: Rankings and visibility start improving.

 Months 3–6: Lead flow increases and calls pick up.

 Months 6–12: Strong market positioning and consistent inbound leads. This is when SEO becomes highly profitable.

 What kind of businesses do you work with?

 We specialize in home service businesses — plumbers, HVAC, roofers, electricians, landscapers, pest control, and similar trades.

 We work best with established companies doing $1.5M+ in revenue that want more qualified calls and consistent growth.

 Is SEO worth it if I'm already running ads?

 Yes — and they complement each other.

 Ads bring immediate leads but stop when you stop paying. SEO builds a long-term asset that continues generating leads.

 Most clients use both. Over time, SEO becomes the most profitable channel as cost per lead decreases.

 What do you need from me to get started?

 Just a few basics — access to your Google Business Profile, details about your services and service area, and job photos if available.

 We handle execution. You stay focused on running your business.
