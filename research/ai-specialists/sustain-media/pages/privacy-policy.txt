Sustain Media
Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Sustain Media respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information submitted through our website, job applications, and advertising platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

Information We Collect

When you apply for a role with Sustain Media, we may collect information such as:

Name
Email address
Phone number
Country or location
LinkedIn profile
Employment and professional experience
Resume or portfolio information
Answers to application and screening questions
Any other information you voluntarily provide

We may also receive basic technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information.

How We Use Your Information

We use applicant information to:

Review and evaluate job applications
Determine whether you may be a fit for a role
Contact you about your application
Schedule interviews or request additional information
Maintain recruiting records
Improve our recruiting process

Submitting an application does not create an employment or contractor relationship.

How We Store and Share Information

We may store applicant information using business tools such as Google Workspace and other recruiting, communication, or automation software used by Sustain Media.

We may also receive information through platforms such as Meta's Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our recruiting process. We do not sell applicant personal information.

Because Sustain Media operates internationally, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Data Retention

We retain applicant information for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate your application, maintain recruiting records, comply with legal requirements, or consider you for future opportunities.

You can ask us to delete your applicant information at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact:

Sustain Media
Email: team@sustain-media.com

We will respond to reasonable privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.

Third-Party Platforms

If you submit information through Facebook, Instagram, or another third-party platform, that platform may also process your information under its own privacy policy.

Sustain Media is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
