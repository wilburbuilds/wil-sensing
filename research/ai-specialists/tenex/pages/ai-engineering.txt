What we believe
We hire the best

Other agencies make money by hiring cheap talent and charging a premium. We know that one world-class engineer can do more than 10 mediocre engineers, so we only hire the best.

We use AI (a lot)

You’d be surprised how many people still type letters, one at a time, into an IDE. We use all the off-the-shelf AI tools and we build our own tools to get an edge.

We charge on outcomes

We charge our clients based on what we produce, measured by story-points that we define.

We pay on outcomes

We pay our team based on the story-points that they complete. So everyone is aligned on output and speed.

Why you need us

Hiring in-house is slow, risky, and extremely important

Perfect-fit hires are mission-critical but can take months to secure.

Hiring top engineers is more expensive than ever.

Velocity is a critical competitive advantage in this historically dynamic market.

Unwinding bad in-house habits (slow pace, AI-allergic) is extremely difficult.

What we do

We become an extension of your team, building whatever you need.

Application Development

Our team expertly handles comprehensive full-stack application development, from backend APIs to frontend UI design.

Fine-Tuning Models

We empower your team by fine-tuning models to perfectly align with your specific business objectives and use cases.

Code Migration + Refactors

Our specialists efficiently manage language migrations, version upgrades, and strategic codebase restructuring.

Data Engineering + Analysis

We streamline data warehouse migrations, conduct precise data cleaning, and deliver robust preprocessing workflows.

Custom Agentic Solutions

We deliver state-of-the-art, customized agentic and AI-powered solutions tailored specifically to your business needs.

+ More

Flexible software solutions for any team. We are flexible and we work within your existing systems to become a part of your team, seamlessly.

Why work with us

We slot into your workflow for frictionless collaboration.

Dedicated Team

Your team includes a Technical Product Manager & Forward Deployed Engineers, who can support your product & engineering needs, end-to-end.

Sprint Cycles

Every two weeks, you choose which tickets to prioritize.

Project Management

Check-ins

Regular check-ins and calls to review progress, roadmap, and next steps.

Ticket Submission

Code Submission
