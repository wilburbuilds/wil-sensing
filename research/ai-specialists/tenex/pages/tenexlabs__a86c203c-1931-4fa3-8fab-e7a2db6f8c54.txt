Title: Senior AI Strategist, Process @ Tenex
Meta description: Tenex builds frontier AI that works in the real world. Our strategists sit with C-suite leaders, find where AI adds the most value, and drive it into production.

We were founded in New York City in 2025. We are currently 70 people and will reach 140 by the end of the year. We build for Fortune 500 companies across media, healthcare, retail, finance, and sports.

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Cursor build the frontier. As their trusted implementation partner, we turn technology into reliable systems businesses use every day.

 


THE ROLE

You’ll own client workstreams end to end: find where AI adds real value, define what gets built, drive adoption, and keep proving out the business results. You’ll report to an Engagement Manager and will partner with a Forward Deployed Engineer who owns architecture and delivery.

We look for strategists who:

 - Have changed how a team works, and can explain what they changed, why, and what happened.

 - Turn unclear business problems into requirements, priorities, and a plan an engineer can act on.

 - Can explain technical tradeoffs to a CEO

 - Use AI tools daily to solve problems

You will learn to run diagnostics that surface real value drivers, scope engagements an engineer can build against, and manage transformation rollouts and adoption.

The strategists we put in front of clients are the product, so hiring is the most important decision we make each week. Our strategists have worked at firms like McKinsey, Bain, Deloitte, EY, PWC, Accenture, and have founded AI consultancies of their own.

 


RECENT WORK

 - Four-week diagnostic across claims, underwriting, and customer operations for a national insurer. We identified the ten highest-return AI opportunities, presented a roadmap, and delivered the first two.

 - Two-week sprint to define an agent’s requirements for a healthcare system. We Prototyped it with engineering and managed its rollout across three hospitals.

 - AI training program built across a private equity firm’s portfolio. We developed the playbook, led training, and prepared internal champions to carry the work forward after the engagement ended.

 


HOW WE PAY

Base $130K-$180K • Quarterly bonus $5K-$15K

 


HOW WE WORK

Tenex is intense by design. The pace is fast, and the standards are high. People share what they learn, challenge each other, and step in to help, so the bar is high and so is the support.

 

This is probably not the right place if you want a narrow scope, a predictable pace, or distance from customers. It is built for strategists who want responsibility, hard problems, and a steep learning curve. We work from our New York City office five days a week and travel to client sites about once a month

 


OUR VISION

We’re bootstrapped and profitable, and we reinvest what we earn into tools, research, and products of our own. Over time, that’s how we build a modern-day Bell Labs: a place where solving today’s problems gives us the means to tackle bigger ones.

 


BENEFITS

 - Medical insurance with PPO and HSA options, plus dental and vision

 - HSA, Healthcare FSA, and commuter benefits

 - 100% employer-paid life and AD&D insurance

 - 100% employer-paid long-term disability insurance

 - Responsible PTO: take what you need

 


WORKING AT TENEX

We work from our New York City office five days a week. Expect to travel to client sites approximately once a month.
