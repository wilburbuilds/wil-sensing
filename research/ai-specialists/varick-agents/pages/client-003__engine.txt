CHAPTER 04
How agents actually work

A supply chain disruption arrives. One Main Operations Agent spawns 5 specialized sub-agents in parallel. Each assembles different context, checks different thresholds, and surfaces different decisions. This is the complexity the platform absorbed so the team no longer had to.
