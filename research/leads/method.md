# What worked in batch 1

Date of the pass: 2026-10-26.

## Signals that produced leads

The reliable pair was a company career page and an about page on the same site. The career page had to name the manual work (dispatch, scheduling, invoicing, AP/AR, intake, data entry). The about page had to name the owner, founder, or president. That pair is most of the fit-5 and fit-4 rows.

A tool name on the posting made the job easier to measure without turning the lead into a software sale. Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, Sage 100, and SimplePractice all showed up as the system a person still operates. Those shops stayed in the list. A posting that asked for Salesforce, SAP, Oracle, or NetSuite was treated as the wrong stack and left out.

X was useful when the account was the business and the post was a hire for a coordination role. Pihl Excavating, Hughes & Cozad, and S&S Concrete Floors were confirmed on their own sites after the X batch. Pain posts from owners who are already running assistants (Suite 200 Grille, Brushworks) fit the monthly service, not a first build, and they scored lower.

University and association job boards (Cal State East Bay, Simmons, PECAA) were worth opening when they named the employer, the date, and the duty. They are copies, so the company site still had to confirm the person.

## Signals that did not

Upwork and "help me automate / Zapier / Make" posts were vendors or anonymous clients. No company name, no lead.

Reddit threads about spreadsheets and dispatch did not open (403 or timeouts). Snippets named no company. They are not a source until a page opens.

BBB, Indeed, Glassdoor, LinkedIn, and the California license lookup often returned 403 or 401. A snippet from those domains was not used. When Indeed did open, the useful fact was sometimes "this job has expired."

Vendor case studies (ServiceTitan, BILL, Sage, Praxedo, Granum) described a manual process that had already been replaced. They read like leads and are not current ones.

Accounts selling automation, including CableFi and Blue Collar OS, were excluded. A perfect pain sentence that ends in a product link is a competitor.

JavaScript shells were a practical failure. Ironstone's team page returned 200 and the owner name was not in the HTML. Those stayed unfinished rather than getting a name from a search snippet.

## How to spend the next batch

Start with the unfinished list in `CONTINUE.md`: the companies where one of the two pages already opened. Then look for new company career pages, city by city. Do not repeat the 64 X queries. Do not go back to Upwork unless the client is already named.
