A U.S. delivery orchestration platform managing enterprise last-mile fulfillment. Billing complexity across shippers and logistics providers outgrew the legacy system — the team needed a purpose-built Freight Audit & Pay platform for multi-party invoicing, cost calculations, and dispute management at scale.
Limestone's AI-augmented velocity pod took full ownership of delivery, shipping a production-grade replacement integrated into the client's existing infrastructure.
A U.S.-based delivery orchestration platform helping enterprises manage last-mile fulfillment with a connected courier network and real-time visibility.

Challenge
Financial-grade logic at scale, without breaking what works. A new invoicing system had to ship fast, stay auditable, and never break the workflows already running underneath it.
Multi-party billing at scale

WHAT WE DID
Solution
The Velocity Framework: six steps, every feature, every time.
Invoice Generation
Traditional invoices for shippers and reverse invoices for logistics providers, integrating directly with delivery data.
Cost Calculation Engine
Complex cost-targeting logic with stored properties, automatic recalculation on delivery changes, and support for Traditional and PostAdjustment invoice types.
Complex cost-targeting logic with stored properties, automatic recalculation on delivery changes.
Dispute Management
Full dispute workflow with status tracking, partial approvals, and resolution flows built from real auditor feedback.
Approval Workflows
Configurable approval windows with Kubernetes CronJobs that automatically process expired invoices daily.
Reporting & Export
Detailed invoice reporting with configurable columns, CSV export, and full transparency into dispute values.
Event-Driven Architecture
Event-driven state changes keep downstream systems synchronized with billing activity in real time.
Cursor
Primary development environment with Plan Mode.
Claude Opus
Primary model for spec writing, plan generation, and code implementation.
