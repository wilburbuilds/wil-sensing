Loan Assessment, Automated End to End
How mBanc replaced a failing vendor and automated application processing, with a 7-developer Limestone team, in a one-year engagement.
FinTech
Mortgage Lending
Managed Delivery
Industries

ABOUT THE PROJECT
Project overview
Engagement
June 2019 — 2020
Team
7 developers, UI/UX designer
Core technologies
ASP.NET, React.js
mBanc is a US non-QM mortgage lender: loans for borrowers who don't qualify through traditional methods. Applications come in online at high monthly volume, and manual assessment had become the constraint on capacity. Their previous vendor couldn't deliver the automation they needed.
Limestone took over in June 2019, rebuilt the landing pages first, then automated the pipeline: questionnaire, document upload and verification, and automatically calculated rates, credit score, and decision, all delivered inside a one-year engagement.
The Challenge
Volume was growing. The manual work wasn't scaling.
A vendor that couldn't deliver
The previous software partner couldn't build what mBanc needed. Limestone arrived with trust to earn.
Manual assessment at volume
Every application meant hands-on work. Capacity to process online applications was capped by people, not demand.
First impressions losing borrowers
The landing pages had to be rebuilt so the company made a credible first impression on potential customers.
Data scattered across systems
Financial statements, bank data, and third-party sources all had to be pulled, parsed, and reconciled per applicant.
WHAT WE BUILT
Solution
A pipeline that qualifies borrowers on its own. An applicant fills out the questionnaire, uploads documents, and gets rates calculated from their own data. mBanc gets a credible site and a system that processes applications automatically.
Loan Application Portal
An online questionnaire that shows applicants which loans they qualify for, with application status visible throughout.
Automated Assessment
The questionnaire flow eliminates the manual side of client assessment entirely.
Document Processing
Upload and verification, with a parsing server that retrieves financial statements and related data from the applicant's bank, plus PDF parsing.
Scoring & Decisioning
Credit score, loan rates, and mBanc's decision calculated automatically from the submitted data.
Third-Party Integrations
APIs pulling data from a range of external applications for a complete financial picture per applicant.
UI/UX Redesign
The platform's look and feel reconceptualized: consistent, intuitive, and starting with landing pages that convert.
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
Built to maximize application throughput.
The parsing server retrieves financial statements and related information from the applicant's bank. Using that data plus everything submitted in the questionnaire, the mBanc portal aggregates and displays application status, credit score, and loan conditions for each applicant, with queues, blob storage, and databases keeping the pipeline resilient at volume.
INSIDE mbanc
Questionnaire in, pre-approval out.
The borrower flow from questionnaire to credit check to pre-approved rates, and the loan dashboard where payments, documents, and balances live.
