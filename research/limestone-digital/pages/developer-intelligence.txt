FAQ
What tech leaders ask about Dev Intelligence.
What sources does it support?
Version control: GitHub and Bitbucket. Issue tracking: Jira and ClickUp. AI usage: Cursor, Anthropic, and OpenAI. Three sources, one read.
Does it require code changes?
No. Read-only integrations with your providers. Nothing installs on developer machines, nothing touches your pipelines.
What will our security team want to know?
Access is read-only and least-privilege, scoped per source, and revocable any time. Data stays within the platform’s access controls. We’ll walk your CISO through the integration scopes before anything connects.
How fresh is the data?
Daily activity, read weekly. The pulse is deliberately a weekly rhythm: enough signal to act on, not enough noise to ignore.
Is it standalone or part of an engagement?
Both. DevInt runs inside every Limestone engagement by default, and teams use it standalone as their weekly engineering read.
How is this different from Copilot's dashboard?
Copilot reports seats and acceptance rates for one tool. DevInt resolves activity across your AI providers, version control, and issue tracker into one story per person and one verdict per week. Getting that answer any other way costs a tour of four or five tools.
