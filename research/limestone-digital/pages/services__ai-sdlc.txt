BEFORE WE TALK
Questions worth asking.
What does an AI Velocity Pod include?
A typical starting pod combines one dedicated senior AI engineer with 0.5 FTE of shared advisory, delivery, and specialist support. That support is pooled across the services shown, rather than a separate hire for each. Additional pods can run parallel workstreams.
How much does AI SDLC delivery cost?
A typical AI Velocity Pod is $17K–$20K per month, depending on scope and the support required. Additional pods are priced into the proposal when parallel delivery is needed. The proposal also sets out expected model, infrastructure, and ongoing support costs. Engagements are month-to-month, with scope, capacity, and responsibilities agreed in the Statement of Work.
How does the performance guarantee work?
No measured uplift, no invoice. Before work starts, we agree the baseline, success measures, assessment period, and guarantee conditions in writing. The proposal sets out how performance is assessed and how the guarantee applies to payment.
How quickly can we start?
Our typical journey from first conversation through discovery, agreement, and start is three to six weeks. Much of that time depends on client review and procurement. We confirm availability, access requirements, and the start date for your scope.
Who sets priorities and accepts the work?
Your sponsor and product or technical lead set business priorities and accept releases. The pod handles delivery and coordination. Documentation, pairing, and handover help your team maintain the software.
Can AI SDLC support new products and older codebases?
Yes. We build new products, extend live platforms, and modernize established software. A vendor transition starts with a review of code, access, documentation, and current commitments.
What happens after we book a call?
We discuss your goal, team, and constraints, then involve a technical lead to assess the approach. The first conversations help both sides decide whether there is a fit. Detailed discovery is a separate next step: we agree its scope before proceeding, then use the findings to prepare the delivery proposal.
What does detailed discovery involve?
For a build or delivery workstream, discovery typically takes one to two weeks, with up to three working sessions and review of relevant material. We send agendas in advance. Your team keeps the Vision and Scope document and Data Map whether or not you proceed. Delivery scope, acceptance criteria, and monthly price are agreed before you commit to the build.
How do you handle confidential information?
We put a mutual NDA in place before exchanging sensitive information. Early exploration can use scrubbed or synthetic samples. Your security and technical owners review access, data flows, and the proposed tools; any required data-handling agreement is signed before access to regulated data.
What is an AI SDLC?
A software development lifecycle that uses AI in planning, implementation, testing, and review. Engineers remain responsible for requirements, architecture, code quality, and approval.
