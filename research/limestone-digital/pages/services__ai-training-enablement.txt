BEFORE WE TALK
Questions worth asking.
How does AI enablement help executives and business teams?
Leaders work on opportunities, investment decisions, and oversight. Business teams practice on their own reporting, research, and recurring tasks. No coding background is required for these groups.
How much time will our people need?
Choose two dedicated days or sessions spread over two working weeks. Both include demonstrations, assignments, coaching, and assessment. For the distributed format, the proposal confirms session length and practice time before you commit.
How much does AI enablement cost?
The foundation engagement is typically under $10K, depending on scope. It covers a defined program of preparation, practical sessions, assessment, and handover. The proposal confirms participants, format, support, and fees. Production agents or additional engineering work are priced separately.
How does the performance guarantee work?
No measured uplift, no invoice. For enablement, the agreed uplift is assessed at day 60. We document the baseline, success measures, guarantee conditions, and payment arrangements before the engagement starts.
What happens after we book a call?
We discuss the team you want to equip, the work it handles, and the tools it uses. Then we recommend a starting group and format and prepare the scope and price.
Do we need to change our AI tools?
Not necessarily. We assess your existing tools and information-handling requirements. Technical and security colleagues join decisions about access or integrations when needed.
How do you handle confidential information?
We put a mutual NDA in place before exchanging sensitive information. Early exploration can use scrubbed or synthetic samples. Your security and technical owners review access, data flows, and the proposed tools; any required data-handling agreement is signed before access to regulated data.
What happens to the use cases we identify?
We prioritize them by impact, feasibility, and data readiness. Your team chooses which to pursue. Agent builds can be delivered by an AI Velocity Pod in a separate engagement; there is no obligation to proceed.
What happens when the engagement ends?
Your team keeps the assessment, working examples, guidance, and prioritized use cases. Internal champions help colleagues continue applying the practices. Additional support can be scoped separately.
What is AI enablement?
A practical program that helps people use AI in their work. Limestone combines demonstrations, coached assignments, proficiency assessment, champion identification, and use case prioritization.
