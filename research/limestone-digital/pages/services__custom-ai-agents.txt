BEFORE WE TALK
Questions worth asking.
What makes a good first project?
Choose a repeated task or product capability with a clear owner and a way to judge the result. We assess the volume, available information, integration needs, and exceptions before recommending a build. New products and established systems are both in scope.
How much do custom AI agents cost?
A typical AI Velocity Pod is $17K–$20K per month, depending on scope and the support required. Additional pods are priced into the proposal when parallel delivery is needed. The proposal also sets out expected model, infrastructure, and ongoing support costs. Agent builds use the same pod model as our AI SDLC service. The monthly pod price is not a fixed price for the whole project.
How does the performance guarantee work?
No measured uplift, no invoice. Before work starts, we agree the baseline, success measures, assessment period, and guarantee conditions in writing. The proposal sets out how performance is assessed and how the guarantee applies to payment.
What happens after we book a call?
We discuss your goal, team, and constraints, then involve a technical lead to assess the approach. The first conversations help both sides decide whether there is a fit. Detailed discovery is a separate next step: we agree its scope before proceeding, then use the findings to prepare the delivery proposal.
What does detailed discovery involve?
For a build or delivery workstream, discovery typically takes one to two weeks, with up to three working sessions and review of relevant material. We send agendas in advance. Your team keeps the Vision and Scope document and Data Map whether or not you proceed. Delivery scope, acceptance criteria, and monthly price are agreed before you commit to the build.
Can custom AI agents work with scattered data or existing software?
Yes, subject to an assessment of access and data quality. Discovery maps the sources, business rules, and missing information. We identify the necessary data and integration work before committing to a build.
How do you handle incorrect answers or actions?
We test against cases your specialists have checked, validate outputs, and define when an action needs approval or handover. Monitoring and evaluation continue after launch.
How do you handle confidential information?
We put a mutual NDA in place before exchanging sensitive information. Early exploration can use scrubbed or synthetic samples. Your security and technical owners review access, data flows, and the proposed tools; any required data-handling agreement is signed before access to regulated data.
Who maintains the system after launch?
Limestone can operate it or hand it to your team. We agree support responsibilities, documentation, and running costs as part of the proposal.
What are custom AI agents?
Software built to carry out tasks using your business data and tools. An agent can follow a defined workflow, take permitted actions, and hand exceptions to a person.
Need a different kind of support?
Two other ways we work with teams: AI enablement to build the skill in-house, and AI SDLC to speed up delivery with an embedded pod.
