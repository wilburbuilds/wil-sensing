About
Limestone Digital
Limestone Digital is an AI transformation and embedded engineering partner for enterprises and private equity portfolio companies. We build custom AI agents, accelerate software delivery, and train teams to adopt AI.

Our engineers work inside your existing systems, from data preparation and integration through production deployment. We serve businesses in healthcare, insurance, lending, financial services, and logistics. Your team owns the systems we build and develops the capability to run them.

Borys Gurtovyi
Partner, Healthcare and Agentic Systems
San Francisco, USA
Borys Gurtovyi is Partner, Healthcare and Agentic Systems at Limestone Digital.
He is an engineer and founder who builds agentic AI that runs in production. He is the founder of Reclaimly, a healthcare AI company where six specialized LLM workers place real calls to patients about medical billing across three model providers, with PHI redaction, full observability, and human escalation at every layer. Before Reclaimly he spent years as a senior iOS engineer at Tinder through its highest-scale period, served as a CTO in logistics, and earned a computer science degree from the University of Southern California. One rule runs through everything he builds: the model is the commodity, the context is the moat.
Key focus areas
Agentic AI systems where the agent is the product, not a copilot, with every action governed and auditable
Production healthcare AI under HIPAA: PHI/PII redaction, audit trails, and human escalation built in from the first layer
Multi-provider LLM infrastructure: intelligent routing across model vendors, full observability, multi-tenant architecture
Taking agentic systems from pilot to production inside regulated, high-stakes environments
