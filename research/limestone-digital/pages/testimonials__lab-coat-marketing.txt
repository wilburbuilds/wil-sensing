Services Provided
Development
Lab Coat Marketing needed to decide how to build a software product while working under tight timelines and real delivery pressure. They had experience working both with internal developers and external teams, and understood the trade-offs — hiring a single developer meant relying on one perspective, while they had a clear vision of what they wanted to build. They chose Limestone Digital based on prior experience and early alignment around that vision, including help in defining the right technology approach and team. Over time, the Limestone team became fully integrated into their workflow, operating not as an external vendor, but as part of their internal team.
Joe Evans & Chris Carroll
Founders, Lab Coat Marketing
The Limestone team allows you to be as involved as you want to be — while still having the support in place to get things done
— Joe Evans,
Co-Founder & CMO, Lab Coat Marketing
Engagement overview
Lab Coat Marketing partnered with Limestone Digital to build a custom reservation and rental platform for the self-storage industry, working with third-party management software APIs and delivering a specific user experience without internal development capabilities. The engagement involved executing a software initiative outside the company’s core expertise, under tight timelines and with business impact tied directly to the outcome. Instead of relying on a single internal hire, the team worked with Limestone Digital to access a broader engineering capability and ensure continuity beyond individual contributors.
