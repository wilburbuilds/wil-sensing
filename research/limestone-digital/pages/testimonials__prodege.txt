Development
Prodege needed a way to support its B2B platforms without pulling focus from core consumer products. What started as a cautious outsourcing decision quickly evolved into a low-touch, fully managed collaboration. With Limestone Digital taking ownership of delivery and maintenance, internal involvement dropped significantly while systems remained consistently up to date. Over time, the partnership proved reliable not just in execution, but in reducing operational overhead and simplifying day-to-day management.
Shane O’Neill
CTO, Prodege
As time went on, it was almost zero involvement from me because they were managing everything — just a check-in once in a while to make sure everything was going fine.
— Shane O’Neill
, Prodege
Engagement overview
Prodege partnered with Limestone Digital to support the development and maintenance of its B2B web platforms, allowing internal teams to stay focused on core consumer products. Limestone took full ownership of delivery and ongoing updates, operating with minimal oversight and a proactive approach to system maintenance.
In parallel, they supported team scaling through a structured recruitment process, providing pre-vetted engineers who became productive quickly. The collaboration also included solving complex technical and operational challenges, enabling consistent delivery without disrupting business operations.

About the client — Prodege
Prodege is a global consumer marketing and insights platform built on a network of proprietary loyalty programs and engaged user communities. It enables brands and agencies to gather real-time consumer feedback, access targeted audiences, and drive customer acquisition through data-driven engagement.
By combining performance marketing with large-scale consumer insights, Prodege helps organizations better understand behavior, optimize campaigns, and scale growth.
