Testimonial: Sentinel Systems
A closer look at how our collaboration helped this company achieve meaningful results.
Company
Sentinel Systems
Dates
03/2022 – Present
Services Provided
UI/UX
Development
Sentinel Systems needed to transition from a legacy Windows-based platform to a fully cloud-based solution to keep up with evolving technology. Having worked with Limestone Digital before, they already had a positive experience, which made it the first team they turned to when defining the next step. The decision was driven by alignment in how both sides approach partnerships — focusing not only on delivery, but on understanding business goals and working toward them together. Prior collaboration, clear communication, and relevant experience in cloud development made Limestone a confident and natural choice.
Dee Jay Skinner
Chief Technology Officer
I want to focus on the project, not on managing developers. Limestone handles everything around the team, which makes my job much easier.
— Dee Jay Skinner,
Chief Technology Officer
Engagement overview
The engagement focused on modernizing Sentinel’s existing product and supporting its transition from a legacy desktop system toward a cloud-based architecture. Limestone Digital worked closely with a small internal team, taking ownership of development, team coordination, and delivery processes, allowing the client to stay focused on product direction rather than day-to-day execution.
The collaboration evolved over time, with the team scaling from a small setup to a larger structure. Limestone ensured continuity throughout by managing team changes and maintaining stable delivery, while helping improve operational efficiency and enabling faster release cycles.

About the client — Sentinel Systems
Sentinel Systems
is a provider of access control and management software for the self-storage industry, with over 35 years of experience. The company develops gate access systems alongside software that enables facility operators to manage tenant access, reservations, payments, and unit inventory. Their solutions are designed to support the day-to-day operations and security of self-storage facilities. Sentinel’s products are used by storage operators to control facility access and manage customer activity across their properties. The company focuses on providing reliable systems that support ongoing operations and help ensure consistent performance in real-world environments.
